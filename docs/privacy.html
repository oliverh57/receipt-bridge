Receipt Bridge

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Receipt Bridge is a Mac app made by Oliver Hynds. It finds receipts in your email and files them to your FreeAgent account. This page explains what it does with your data.

The short version

Receipt Bridge runs on your Mac. There is no Receipt Bridge server: your emails, receipts and account details stay on your Mac and are never sent to us.

Your Google data

When you connect Gmail, Receipt Bridge asks Google for permission to:

  • Read your email, so it can search for receipts from the suppliers you choose and read those emails and their attachments.
  • Label your email, so an email whose receipt has been filed is marked "Receipt Bridge/Filed as Expense" or "Receipt Bridge/Filed as bank transaction". Undo removes the label.

It doesn't send, delete or change your emails in any other way.

Receipt Bridge keeps copies of the receipt emails it finds, and the key Google gives it to reach your mailbox, in its folder on your Mac. The key file can only be read by your Mac user account.

Your Google data is only passed on when you file a receipt: the receipt and its details (supplier, date, amount, VAT) go to your own FreeAgent account. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Receipt Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, your Google data is:

  • used only to find and file your receipts;
  • never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than your own FreeAgent account;
  • never read by a person, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Other services Receipt Bridge connects to

  • FreeAgent, to read your bank feed and categories and to save the receipts and expense claims you file.
  • Suppliers' websites, to download a receipt that an email links to.
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates.
  • HMRC, to fetch the monthly exchange rates for receipts in other currencies.
  • Banks' websites, to show each bank's logo.
  • The senders of emails you open, when an email shows pictures from the web.

Photos of receipts you send from your iPhone are saved to the folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive.

Removing your data

To disconnect Gmail, sign out in Receipt Bridge's Settings. This also cancels its access with Google. You can also remove it from your Google Account's third-party connections. To remove everything Receipt Bridge has stored, uninstall it and delete its folder.
